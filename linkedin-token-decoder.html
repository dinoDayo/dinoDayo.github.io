LinkedIn Token Decoder

Decodes the persistent tracking token LinkedIn stores in your browser's localStorage and shows when LinkedIn first set it. All processing happens in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

How to retrieve your token
  1. Open Chrome and navigate to https://www.linkedin.com. You do not need to log in.
  2. Open DevTools by pressing Cmd + Option + I on Mac or F12 on Windows.
  3. Click the Application tab in the DevTools panel. If you do not see it, click the » overflow arrow at the right end of the tab bar.
  4. In the left sidebar, expand Local Storage and click https://www.linkedin.com.
  5. Find the row where the Key column shows f. Click the row to select it.
  6. Click into the Value column, select all (Cmd+A or Ctrl+A), copy, and paste into the box below.

If there is no row with key f, LinkedIn may not have set a persistent token in your browser yet, or your storage has been cleared since your last visit.

To find this value: open Chrome → DevTools (Cmd+Option+I) → Application → Local Storage → https://www.linkedin.com → key: f
